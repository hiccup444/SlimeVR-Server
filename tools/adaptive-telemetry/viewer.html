Input format

Recordings use a header line, then {type:"frame", droppedFrames, frame:{timestampNanos, samples, computedSamples, resetEpoch, footContacts, trackerPredictions}}. Samples contain id, name, status, raw and adjusted quaternion objects, optional position, temperatureCelsius, angularSpeedRadiansPerSecond, derivedLinearVelocity, and optional measurement-confidence diagnostics. Tracker predictions contain expected and measured rotations plus a shortest-arc residual vector, magnitude, directional consistency, duration, and evidence label. These expected rotations may be conditioned on measured tracking and are not independent truth. Drift residuals can include a contextual pose-confidence learning gate. Pose diagnostics report measurement confidence; older recordings labeled this globalConfidence. Foot contacts and activity fields are diagnostic estimates, not proven accuracy, raw IMU pose, or a solver rerun. A reset epoch or missing sample creates a visible gap.
